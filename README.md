1. Print all four parts.
2. Remove supports and sand the surfaces.
3. Join the upper and lower arm sections with [glue / screws / hinge pins].
4. Attach the fingers and the glove top detail.
5. Finish with primer and paint (see below).

---

## 🎨 Finishing Tips

- Sand progressively (200 → 400 → 800 grit).
- Apply filler primer to hide layer lines.
- Use metallic silver or gunmetal paint, then a dark wash for panel lines.
- Finish with a matte or satin clear coat.
